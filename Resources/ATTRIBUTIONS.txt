VOCABULARY BASE
HearMandarin (https://hearmandarin.com), HSK 3.0 Word List Dataset.
https://hearmandarin.com/datasets/
CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Dataset version: 2026-09-09; generated 2026-09-28; retrieved 2026-10-05.
Adaptations: levels 1–6 selected, global serials converted to level-local,
previous reviewed HSK4 pronunciation/meaning corrections retained, weight
sense of HSK4 两2 clarified. The resulting adapted vocabulary pack is shared
under CC BY-SA 4.0 because retained corrections are CC-CEDICT-informed.
Original base data remains available under CC BY 4.0 from HearMandarin.

https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cedict
CC BY-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
MandarinBean original list: https://mandarinbean.com/new-hsk-4-word-list/
Existing user migration copy preserves that source and previous review.

OFFICIAL HEADWORD CHECK
Chinese Testing International, 新版HSK考试大纲（词汇、汉字、语法）.
https://www.chinesetest.cn/syllabus
Official PDF downloaded 2026-10-05 from hsk.cn-bj.ufileos.com/3.0/.
Machine-readable secondary crosscheck: chelsea6502/hsk-2025-data
https://github.com/chelsea6502/hsk-2025-data
That crosscheck is not redistributed as app data.

EXAMPLES
Tatoeba contributors, selected bilingual Mandarin/English pairs by Charles
Kelly / ManyThings.org: https://www.manythings.org/anki/
https://tatoeba.org/
CC BY 2.0 France: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/
Each included sentence retains both contributor usernames and sentence IDs,
shown in the word card and stored in words.json. Selected from the bilingual
download; Chinese converted to simplified and sentence Pinyin generated
using macOS Mandarin-to-Latin transliteration. Automatic sentence Pinyin is
labelled because polyphonic words and neutral tones may need checking.
Examples can use a different sense from a word's teaching gloss.
CíBar original examples are labelled separately and dedicated under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
No audio or dictionary corpus is bundled. No endorsement by any source is
implied. DATA-REVIEW.json and HSK4-CORRECTIONS-REVIEW.json describe changes.
